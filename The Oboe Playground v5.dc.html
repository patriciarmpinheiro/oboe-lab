Who is behind this

HELLO,
I’M PATRÍCIA

I play the oboe, break it open, and write down what comes out.

Oboist and cor anglais player working in a broad range from classic music to experimental one— premières, strange fingerings, long afternoons with a microphone and a mountain of reeds. This playground is my open notebook: everything I find, catalogued so you can hear it before you write it. I hope I can share a bit of my enthusiasm for the instrument with you!
