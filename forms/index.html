What this is

Thought of everything the oboe could do, then went looking for the archive?

That is what I am building, and it is being built with the people who wrote the pieces. A list of repertoire for the oboe family that is free, permanent and public, searchable by technique. Your pieces go in and stay in. A composer looking for pieces that use multiphonics finds yours. An oboist looking for something new to play finds yours.

Instruments
Oboe, oboe d'amore, cor anglais, bass oboe, musette
Forces
Solo, with piano, duo, trio, wind quintet, double reeds, chamber, orchestra, electronics. Any formation that includes one of the instruments above
Idiom
Contemporary concert music, jazz, film, games, ambient, improvised
Status
Published, self-published, unpublished. All the same to me
How many
Up to six pieces in one go. One form, one send
What it is not
A competition. No jury, no fee, no ranking
